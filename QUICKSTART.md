# Quick Start Guide - Moodle Job Portal

Get started with the Job Portal in 5 minutes!

## For Students

### 1. Access the Job Portal
- Log into your Moodle account
- Navigate to the Job Portal (link should be in your menu or dashboard)
- Or go directly to: `https://yourmoodle.com/local/jobportal/`

### 2. Browse Jobs
- View all available job listings on the main page
- Use the search box to find specific jobs
- Click on any job to see full details

### 3. Create Your Profile (First Time)
- Click the **"My Profile"** button
- Fill in:
  - Your skills (e.g., "Python, JavaScript, Communication")
  - Work experience or internships
  - Educational background
  - Portfolio URL (if you have one)
- Upload your resume (PDF, DOC, or DOCX - max 5MB)
- Click **"Save changes"**

### 4. Apply for a Job
- Click on a job you're interested in
- Click **"Apply Now"**
- Write a cover letter explaining why you're a good fit
- Upload your resume (or use the one from your profile)
- Click **"Apply"**
- Done! You'll see a confirmation message

### 5. Track Your Applications
- Click **"My Applications"** to see all jobs you've applied for
- Check the status:
  - 🟡 **Pending** - Application submitted, waiting for review
  - 🔵 **Reviewed** - Employer has reviewed your application
  - 🟢 **Accepted** - Congratulations! You're shortlisted
  - 🔴 **Rejected** - Not selected this time, keep applying!

### Tips for Students
✅ Complete your profile before applying  
✅ Customize each cover letter for the specific job  
✅ Keep your resume updated and professional  
✅ Apply before the deadline  
✅ Check your applications regularly for status updates

## For Administrators/Managers

### 1. Access Job Management
- Log in with administrator/manager privileges
- Navigate to the Job Portal

### 2. Post a New Job
- Click **"Post a Job"** button
- Fill in job details:
  - **Job Title**: e.g., "Software Developer Intern"
  - **Company**: Your organization name
  - **Description**: Detailed job description (supports formatting)
  - **Location**: e.g., "Chennai, India" or "Remote"
  - **Job Type**: Select from dropdown (Full-time, Part-time, Internship, etc.)
  - **Salary**: Optional, e.g., "₹3-5 LPA" or "Competitive"
  - **Requirements**: List qualifications needed
  - **Deadline**: Set application closing date (optional)
  - **Status**: Check "Active" to publish immediately
- Click **"Save changes"**

### 3. Manage Existing Jobs
- Click on any job posting
- Click **"Edit Job"** to modify details
- Update any information and save

### 4. Review Applications
- Click on a job posting
- Click **"View Applications"** button
- You'll see:
  - Applicant name and email
  - Cover letter
  - Application date
  - Current status
- For each application, you can:
  - **Accept** - Shortlist the candidate
  - **Reject** - Decline the application
  - **Mark as Reviewed** - Note that you've seen it

### 5. Manage Application Statuses
- Update statuses as you progress through hiring:
  1. **Pending** → **Reviewed** (after initial screening)
  2. **Reviewed** → **Accepted** (for shortlisted candidates)
  3. Any status → **Rejected** (if not proceeding)

### Best Practices for Administrators
✅ Write clear, detailed job descriptions  
✅ Set realistic application deadlines  
✅ Review applications promptly  
✅ Update statuses to keep students informed  
✅ Provide helpful feedback when possible

## Common Questions

### Can I apply for the same job twice?
No, the system prevents duplicate applications. You can only apply once per job.

### Can I edit my application after submitting?
Currently, no. Make sure to review everything before clicking "Apply". This feature may be added in future versions.

### What file formats are accepted for resumes?
PDF (.pdf), Microsoft Word (.doc, .docx). Maximum file size is 5MB.

### Can I withdraw my application?
Contact your administrator if you need to withdraw an application.

### How do I know if my application status changes?
Check "My Applications" regularly. (Future versions may include email notifications)

### Can I see who applied for my posted job?
Yes, if you have administrator/manager permissions, click "View Applications" on any job you posted.

### Can I edit a job after posting it?
Yes, administrators can edit job details at any time by clicking "Edit Job".

### How do I make a job inactive without deleting it?
Edit the job and uncheck the "Active" status. The job will be hidden from students but data is preserved.
